Topic-filter labelling rubric — economics in r/changemyview
A thread is labelled economics if its main claim (what the OP wants their view changed about) substantively concerns one or more of:

Prices, wages, income, or compensation
Taxes, welfare, subsidies, or government spending
Markets, trade, or competition
Employment, unemployment, or labour
Fiscal policy, monetary policy, inflation, or interest rates
Economic inequality or wealth distribution
Specific economic systems (capitalism, socialism, communism, etc.)
Cost-of-living, housing affordability, healthcare costs as economic problems
Industry-specific economic claims (e.g. "the music industry exploits artists" → economics)

A thread is labelled not economics if its main claim concerns:

Politics without economic substance (e.g. immigration framed as identity, abortion, gun rights without economic angle)
Social or cultural issues (relationships, parenting, ethics, religion)
Philosophy, morality, or epistemology without economic substance
Personal or interpersonal claims
Aesthetics, entertainment, technology preferences

Edge cases:

If economics is mentioned but isn't the OP's main claim → not economics. (Example: "I think the death penalty should be abolished, partly because it's expensive." → not economics; main claim is moral.)
If the thread is ambiguous between two domains (e.g. "rent control is unfair to landlords") → economics, because the policy mechanism is economic even if framed morally.
Healthcare: if about cost or insurance → economics. If about ethics or access without cost framing → not economics.
"Should X be free / paid" questions → economics (about pricing).
Be strict by default: when uncertain, label not economics. The downstream goal is a clean economics subcorpus; false positives in your gold label would inflate the model's apparent accuracy.
